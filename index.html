The Zyphorux Universe

A universe built in layers. Nine realms are stacked and stitched together by veils, and everything inside them rises and falls with one slow tide.

Nothing in this universe is only itself. Every realm, shelf and stone carries a resonance, a fixed signature that decides where it can exist. Two unlike resonances cannot share a place unless a veil stands between them. The whole universe is the sum of those separations.

Its time is the Tide, a swell that rises and falls through every realm. One full rise and fall is a cycle. When the Tide swells, resonance runs strong and crossings open easily. When it ebbs, the realms pull apart and the veils thicken.

The nine realms are numbered from the outermost inward. Thall (03) folded in on itself long ago. Ostrene (06) is the bright realm of the Veilwrights, who built the first crossings. The Between (07) is where Zyphorux lives. Ceruleth (09) is the outermost realm, where the Tide is born.

How the universe is organised

  1. UniverseThe whole lattice of nine realms. Everything that has resonance belongs to it.
  2. RealmOne layer of existence with its own resonance. Realms are numbered 01 to 09.
  3. ReachA region inside a realm. A reach has a name, a climate of light and a shape of its own.
  4. CrossingA rift that has held. It joins one reach to another, sometimes across realms.
  5. VeilA layer inside a crossing. Beyond each veil is a domain, a pocket of space that carries the veil's name.

Example: Zyphorux's domain is the Seventh Veil, inside the Veyra Crossing, in Veyra Reach, in The Between, which is Realm 07 of the Zyphorux Universe.

The Between

Every realm leaks a little at its edges. The Between is the realm those leaks gathered into. It has no sky of its own. Its sky is whichever realm lies nearest, so the light changes with the Tide: pale gold from Ostrene at the swell, deep violet from Ceruleth at the ebb.

Its land is a scattering of shelves, drifting islands of anchorstone that hang in a dark, slow sea. Gravity there runs toward the nearest shelf, so travellers learn to walk on any surface that holds them. Because it belongs to no one, every treaty between realms is signed here, and every realm sends its dead memory here to be kept.

Its people are the Keepers, shelf-dwellers who tend lamps, record echoes and guide visitors across. The Veilwrights of Ostrene come to maintain the crossings. The Echo-readers travel between shelves, reading what the stones remember.

Veyra Crossing

The largest stable rift in the universe, raised in Cycle 218. It has seven veils, each one thinner than the last. The Seventh opens onto the Open Seam itself.

Vaelith Shelf

Zyphorux's home. A shelf anchored around the Open Seam, with tell-lamps along its edge and the Lantern Vault in its underside.

The Choir Stones

A ring of standing stones that sound a low tone when a realm's resonance shifts. Keepers count the tones to forecast the Tide.

The Hollow Sea

The slow dark water between the shelves. It holds light the way other water holds cold, and pools it in places that Echo-readers map.

The Slack

A stretch of the Between where distance does not hold. Ten steps can be a hundred, or none. Zyphorux crosses it without a guide.

Phenomena and resources

Words of this universe

Resonance
The signature carried by everything that exists. It decides where a thing can be.
Veil
A boundary between domains. Veils are numbered, and a higher number means a thinner veil.
Rift
A tear between reaches. Most heal within a pulse. A rift that holds becomes a crossing.
Echo
The recorded remnant of an event, kept in tellglass or in stone.
Cycle
One full rise and fall of the Tide. A cycle holds twenty-four pulses.
Phase
How a thing stands to a veil: present (fully here), veiled (behind a veil) or echoed (remaining only as record).
Drift
The slow movement of shelves across the Hollow Sea.

Zyphorux

Zyphorux belongs to no realm. It is what was left when two of them touched.

Zyphorux is a convergence: a form made of resonance from two realms that met above Veyra for a single pulse in Cycle 7,421. It has no home resonance of its own, so no veil can hold it out and no crossing can hold it in. The ring of black at its chest is its core, a rift that has never opened and never closed.

It is patient and dry, and careful with words. It speaks by resonance, so what it says is felt before it is heard. It collects echoes, listens to the Choir Stones, trims the tell-lamps on Vaelith Shelf and sits at the Seventh Veil during the swell, watching the realms move against each other.

Zyphorux is rarely seen. It arrives when a realm is about to fold, or when a new one is about to begin. The Keepers of Vaelith keep a lamp lit for it, and are usually told nothing in return.

I am not from any one realm. I am from the place where they touched.

How Zyphorux moves through the world

  • PhasingShifts its phase between present and veiled, passing through a veil without opening it.
  • Seam-bendingLoosens or tightens a veil for a short time, enough to let a crossing through or hold one shut.
  • Drinking the swellTakes in the surplus resonance of a high Tide so that crossings do not overflow.
  • Resonance speechSpeaks without sound, to anyone whose resonance is close enough to answer.
  • CrossingTravels between realms with no crossing at all, by stepping through the gap between them.

Home: Vaelith Shelf

Vaelith Shelf is a wide, flat shelf of anchorstone in Veyra Reach, anchored around the Open Seam, a rift that has stayed open since the shelf was settled in Cycle 904. The Seam runs through its centre as a thin line of cold light, and the Keepers built their dwellings in a ring around it.

Spires of dark stone rise from its edge, and tell-lamps hang from each one. A lamp burns steadily when the Tide is calm and flickers when a crossing is about to open. Beneath the shelf lies the Lantern Vault, where the Hollowlight Prism was kept.

Zyphorux lives here because the Seam is the one place in the universe that matches its core. Standing beside it, Zyphorux is never pulled toward any realm.

The Hollowlight Prism

The last complete record of a realm that no longer exists.

Appearance
A tall, double-pointed prism of dark glass. Gold light turns to violet inside it, and three thin rings hang around it, tilting when a veil is near.
Origin
Formed in Cycle 2,650 when Thall folded. Everything the realm had remembered, its places, its voices and its tide-songs, pressed into one piece of glass.
Purpose
It keeps Thall. Anyone who holds it can read the realm's echoes as clearly as living memory.
Location
Kept in the Lantern Vault beneath Vaelith Shelf. Since Cycle 9,046 its place has been empty.
Importance
It is the only full record of Thall. A native who touches it begins to remember Thall in place of themselves, as their resonance retunes toward the fallen realm.
Relation to Zyphorux
Zyphorux has no resonance of its own to lose, so it can hold the Prism unchanged. It reads one echo from it each Tide and has been looking for it since Cycle 9,046.
